Your groups

This page is yours, on this computer alone. On it you see your groups and who is in each, open what the people in them lend you, each in a tab of its own beside this page, open a link to it someone sent in your group’s chat, open each group’s folder, make a group, make a link to invite someone or to add your phone or another computer, bring the apps that come with svrngs to a group, lend your groups what runs on this computer, and choose how many people at once may use what this computer lends them.

A link works for a day, while this computer is on. Making one stops the last link you made letting anyone new in; everyone it let in stays.

A program on this computer, such as one you chat with a friend’s model in, reaches what is lent to you at the address beside it: its Copy button copies it, for you to paste where the program asks for an address.
