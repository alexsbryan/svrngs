Your groups

This page is yours, on this computer alone. On it you see your groups and who is in each, open what the people in them lend you, and choose how many people at once may use what this computer lends them.

Reading your groups…

Done in a terminal

A few things are not done on this page yet. For each, open the Terminal app and type its line, with your own words in place of each <…>:

  • Lend your groups a program this computer runs, such as a model: svrngs share <the number after the colon in its address>, as in svrngs share 8080 for one at http://localhost:8080. svrngs then says what it lent, and to whom.
  • Remove someone from a group: svrngs remove <their name> --group <the group’s name>
  • Invite someone, or add your phone or another computer of yours: svrngs link <the group’s name>, with --device after it for one of yours. It asks that svrngs be stopped first: where it runs in a terminal, with Ctrl-C.
  • Make another group: svrngs new <a name for it>, svrngs stopped first, likewise.

Leaving a group is not possible yet: nothing in svrngs does it.
