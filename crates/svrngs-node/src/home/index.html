Your groups

This page is yours, and only this computer opens it: nobody in your groups sees it. Here you see your groups and who in them is on now, open what they lend you, invite people, and lend what runs on this computer.

A link you make works for a day, while this computer is on. When you make a new one, your last one lets nobody new in; everyone it let in stays.

A program on this computer, such as a chat app, can use what is lent to you too: press “Copy its address for a program” beside it, then paste that where the program asks for an address.
