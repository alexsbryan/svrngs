# svrngs folders — director charter

The supervisor's resolution session decides this queue's forks instead of
waiting on the operator. `NORTH_STAR.md` and `docs/principles/ARCH_PRINCIPLES.md`
are the compass; where they and this charter disagree, they win and the
decision says so. This queue is a spike: it answers whether group folders
work as DESIGN §9 describes them, by a run against bars set before any data,
and it ends in a verdict, not in production. How folders become production
is the operator's, from that verdict (operator, 2026-10-09: "it's still in
spike phase so we can run it in a worktree and assess how to productionize
after").

**Decide:** row order, and splitting or re-scoping a row that cannot
execute; the shape of the folder's act in the record and of the meeting
point's local endpoint; the films the run uses (the first run's, or ones
made the same way, said which); how the run script drives Jellyfin and the
browsers; forking from commonwealth-ai over building new; the smaller
reversible step.

**Bars by their spirit** (operator, 2026-10-08: "the bars are guidance to
align us at the start, the spirit of them are what guides how we
iterate"). Ask what a bar guards and judge by that. A bar is never
rewritten after its data exists: a bar that misses is a miss, said with its
number, and what it would take is a follow-up in the verdict.

**Leave for the operator** (write `NEEDS_HUMAN.md` and stop): merging the
`folders` branch into main, or anything written in the main tree at
`~/dev/svrngs`, where scoped use's loop runs; raising the budget past
15,200, or svrngs-core's past 3,000; changing a bar or a prediction after
gf-0 commits it; the operator's own Jellyfin (its libraries, its
configuration, its data), since the spike runs a Jellyfin of its own;
docker, OrbStack or any tool that is not open software (operator,
2026-10-05: podman and open software only); deploying anything, or any
fetch beyond this host and the relays svrngs already uses; a dependency on
a commonwealth-ai or sovereign crate; anything that needs a service this
host cannot run (say what, and the command that would run it); pushing,
history rewrites, `--no-verify`; anything whose evidence you could not
reproduce.

**Always:** one decision, one commit, with the fork, the choice, the evidence
and what would falsify it in the body.
