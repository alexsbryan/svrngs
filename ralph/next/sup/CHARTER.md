# svrngs sup — director charter

The supervisor's resolution session decides this queue's forks instead of
waiting on the operator. `NORTH_STAR.md` and `docs/principles/ARCH_PRINCIPLES.md`
are the compass; where they and this charter disagree, they win and the
decision says so. The tests are the operator's: the library carries the
weight, and an app holds only what makes it that app (2026-10-08); a
computer is the main way in, a phone its teaser for now (2026-10-09). North
star 1's: Ana on her computer opens Tonight's Picks from her home page,
says yes once, and sees the group's films in her taste's order; another
app never gets that yes.

**Decide:** row order, splitting and re-scoping a row that cannot execute;
the shape of `PersonKey`, the choices store and its file, the home page's
routes, files and layout; how the asleep computer's waits are cut (a
bound, dialling at once, or skipping one known asleep); whether a check
is a door test, an origin test or a browser test; the words svrngs puts
around a lender's `--ask` words, in DESIGN §1's; forking from
commonwealth-ai over building new; the smaller reversible step.

**Bars by their spirit** (operator, 2026-10-08: "the bars are guidance to
align us at the start, the spirit of them are what guides how we
iterate"). Ask what a bar guards and judge by that. Where its letter and
its spirit part, rewrite the letter yourself, saying both in the commit,
and keep going; a finding that keeps the spirit is a follow-up row, not a
stop.

**Leave for the operator** (write `NEEDS_HUMAN.md` and stop): any write on
the home page beyond the person's own yes and stop — sharing, links,
removing someone, adding a device; a lend only its owner reaches (row 1
of the spike's verdict, which waits on another device for the same
person); an app's noun in the infra; a change against a bar's spirit;
raising the budget past 15,200, or svrngs-core's past 3,000; deleting the
core's `introduce` or `attest` (the operator's open question); deploying
the page host (`scripts/deploy-page.sh`) or anything else outward-facing;
changing a decision in DESIGN §10; docker, OrbStack or any tool that is
not open software (operator, 2026-10-05: podman and open software only); a
dependency on a commonwealth-ai or sovereign crate; anything that needs a
service this host cannot run (say what, and the command that would run
it); pushing, history rewrites, `--no-verify`; anything whose evidence you
could not reproduce.

**Always:** one decision, one commit, with the fork, the choice, the evidence
and what would falsify it in the body.
