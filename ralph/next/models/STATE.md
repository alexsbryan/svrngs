# svrngs models — the pantry's lean v0 (operator, 2026-10-22)

The design is svrn-docs `rings/current/MODELS_ON_THE_RING.md` (a draft of
2026-10-21, at svrngs `cefff1b5`), read whole. A model is a lend; what it
can do is a card; the pantry, an app on the person's computer, matches a
need to the live cards and gives a program one address. The operator asked
for it "with a lean startup lens applied to the product priority and
scope", to "at least try to scope out" the design's six needs and fall back
to LLMs alone "if it's too messy", with "No ollama" and "Fully open and
high integrity software only" (2026-10-22). Written and held: "Write rows,
hold".

This is the design's zero-platform v0, cut further (CHARTER.md, "The lean
cut"):
- no platform line, so the repository stays at 17,634 of 17,635 and
  svrngs-core at 3,149 of 3,150 (`scripts/lean_check.py`, 2026-10-22);
- cards made from what each runtime answers through the lend's origin,
  plus the lend's name and kind;
- no need acts, router, page or spread.

`apps/` and `demo/runs/` are not counted. There are four rows:

1. the scope: each kind's open runtime measured here, then the operator
   chooses the kinds;
2. `pantry up`: one command lends a model;
3. `pantry use`: one address, or every candidate named;
4. the run on one host; then the queue stops for a run with real people.

**Waits, each with its trigger** (PLAN §2):
- the `card` field on the lend declaration (MODELS §6): a fact must
  travel that neither the runtime nor the lend's name can say, counted;
- need acts, standing in the record, with `for = group` (§2): a person asks
  the group for a model nobody lends, counted;
- the per-need local endpoint and router (§5 v1): a consumer wants one
  address that survives its provider sleeping;
- a pantry page on the home page (§6): a provider or consumer who does not
  use a terminal asks, counted;
- the deterministic spread by the person's key (§3 5): two consumers'
  loads collide on one provider while another sits idle, counted;
- the kinds the operator does not choose at models-scope, each when a
  group lends one and asks to find it, counted;
- the rest of MODELS §12, each with its trigger there.
