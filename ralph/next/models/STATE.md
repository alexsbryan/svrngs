# svrngs models — the pantry's minimum: two protocols, discovery, one address (operator, 2026-10-22)

The design is svrn-docs `rings/current/MODELS_ON_THE_RING.md` (a draft of
2026-10-21, at svrngs `cefff1b5`). This queue builds far less, by the
operator's ask (2026-10-22): "What is the absolute minimum we can offer that
makes it near obvious for the community to extend and add what they want
near trivially to the architecture? That's the main point for this." Then
"Ok that's pretty good", and "yes, rewrite the queue with that, still
held". Also: "No ollama"; "Fully open and high integrity software only".

**The minimum** (CHARTER.md):
- a lend that answers OpenAI's `/v1/models` is a model, and one that
  answers MCP's `initialize` is a tool;
- `pantry list` shows them;
- `pantry serve` is one local OpenAI-compatible address that routes each
  request by its `model` (prefixed by lender) to the friend who lends it.

Everything else is a server someone writes: a model or tool is lent as it
is, and behaviour is an OpenAI-in, OpenAI-out adapter on the person's own
computer. The vision bridge is the worked example. A provider's recipe is
two shell lines in the README. No platform line: the repository stays at
17,634 of 17,635 and svrngs-core at 3,149 of 3,150
(`scripts/lean_check.py`, 2026-10-22). `apps/` and `demo/runs/` are not
counted.

**The story it is tested against: "Your friends' local AI, in the app you
already use."**
- Ana has a GPU box and lends a strong code model and a vision model. Sam
  lends speech-to-text, a voice and a search tool from a Mac. Bo has a thin
  laptop and runs nothing.
- Bo runs `pantry serve` and points their interface at its one address.
  Their model picker lists `ana/…` and `sam/…`, with nothing registered.
- Bo pastes a screenshot of a broken page and gets a fix from Ana's coder,
  which cannot see. The bridge example on Bo's laptop has Ana's vision
  model describe the screenshot first, and both hops are named.
- Bo talks instead of typing: Sam's speech-to-text, Ana's coder, Sam's
  voice. Bo adds Sam's search tool, and the coder calls it mid-answer as
  Bo.
- Sam closes the lid, and Bo's next voice message names Sam's computer as
  asleep, never quietly another model.
- Cy lends a model through a server the pantry has never heard of, in two
  lines, and it appears in Bo's picker. Dee writes a 50-line adapter. Nobody
  touched the pantry.

**The bars, C1 to C7:**
- C1, discovery by protocol, no registration;
- C2, one address routed by `model`, streamed, for chat, transcription and
  speech;
- C3, extension needs no pantry change;
- C4, composition stays as the person;
- C5, tools by MCP, as the person, their effects listed;
- C6, honest failure, never substituted;
- C7, the lender stays safe: admin routes refused, the door's limit held.

There are four rows:

1. the scope: the story's open servers measured here, then the operator
   chooses;
2. `pantry list` and `pantry serve`;
3. the vision bridge, the worked example of an adapter;
4. the story's run on one host, C1 to C7; then a run with real people.

**Waits, each with its trigger** (PLAN §2):
- `pantry up`, one command to serve and lend: a provider asks for one after
  the run, counted;
- the design's card, facts and matcher (MODELS §3, §6): a person cannot
  choose among their friends' lends by the model picker alone, counted;
- need acts, standing in the record (§2): a person asks the group for a
  model nobody lends, counted;
- one MCP address gathering every friend's tools: a person adds more than a
  handful of tool lends and asks for one, counted;
- several models behind one lend through llama-swap (MIT; measured clean at
  models-scope, but it writes a file path as `model` and adds a supervisor):
  a provider wants several models behind one address, counted;
- a stdio MCP server that knows who is calling: `mcp-proxy` passes none of
  the door's headers (models-scope); a bridge that maps them, when a lent
  stdio tool must act per person, counted;
- per-person permission on a tool beyond the door's who and how many: a
  lender asks to let one friend use a tool that writes and not another;
- a pipeline lent to others, reaching lends on someone else's behalf: the
  operator, since it crosses DESIGN §3's line;
- phones: the address is on the person's loopback; a phone reaches it
  through the room or a carried page, when a person asks, counted;
- a pantry page on the home page: a provider or consumer who does not use
  a terminal asks, counted;
- the deterministic spread by the person's key (§3 5): two consumers' loads
  collide on one provider while another sits idle, counted;
- a wire other than OpenAI's or MCP's (ComfyUI's, SearXNG's own): reachable
  today at its lend's own address; through the pantry when one is asked
  for, counted;
- weights over the ring, budgets, A2A, card signatures, and the rest of
  MODELS §12, each with its trigger there.
