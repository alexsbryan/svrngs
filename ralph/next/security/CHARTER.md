# svrngs security — director charter

The supervisor's resolution session decides this queue's forks instead of
waiting on the operator. `NORTH_STAR.md` and
`docs/principles/ARCH_PRINCIPLES.md` are the compass. Where they and this
charter disagree, they win, and the decision says so.

## What the operator decided, 2026-10-22

- **The rule.** "the consent an app needs is set by what it can do as you,
  not by how it arrived". "libaries are best when they have this capability
  based focused rather than this arbitrary product style bucketing".
- **Explore first.** "I want to explore the implications, fully, before
  hacking on code". The exploration is svrn-docs
  `rings/current/WHAT_CAN_ACT_AS_YOU.md`.
- **Carried apps are trusted with what they read** (that note, §5). The
  containment floor keeps out foreign code and accidental reach. It does not
  stop a pinned app the person chose from sending out what it reads, and the
  docs say exactly that.
- **Queue the security work.** "Agreed. Let's queue the security work." This
  is the note's §6: bugs against svrngs's own invariant, in V1 as released.

## What this queue does not decide

The note's §7 questions are the operator's and stay open here. They are:
- a yes per app for `--reads`;
- whether another member's computer ever speaks for you;
- a yes for a carried app's first version;
- keys everywhere in place of names.

This queue narrows what can be narrowed without them. Above all, it changes
no act format in svrngs-core.

## Decide

- The exact test and fix for each row, within its fence.
- Whether a fix belongs in the door, the fold or the app, by where the
  decision lives already.
- The words a person reads when something is refused.
- The smaller reversible step.

**Bars by their spirit** (operator, 2026-10-08).

## Leave for the operator

Write `NEEDS_HUMAN.md` and stop for:
- **the budget:** past the caps the economizing pass leaves;
- **a fix that would break a path a person uses today**, with no way to
  keep it;
- **a change to what an act is**, or to what an existing group means;
- **any widening of the trust floor**;
- **publishing**, pushing, history rewrites, `--no-verify`;
- **anything whose evidence you could not reproduce.**
