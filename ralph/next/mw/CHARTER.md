# svrngs middleware spike — director charter

The supervisor's resolution session decides this queue's forks instead of
waiting on the operator. `NORTH_STAR.md` and `docs/principles/ARCH_PRINCIPLES.md`
are the compass; where they and this charter disagree, they win and the
decision says so. North star 1's test is this spike's: what does a lazy
person get in ten seconds? A lent Jellyfin opened from the list, playing,
with nothing typed. North star 2 still holds: the infra learns no app, so
whatever Jellyfin needs lives in the program its lender runs.

**Decide:** row order, splitting and re-scoping a row that cannot execute;
the middleware's language, file layout, port, how it keeps the lender's key
and the users it made, held to DESIGN §1's words and §5's rules; forking
from commonwealth-ai over building new; the smaller reversible step; how
the check harness in `apps/jellyfin/check/` is extended.

**Leave for the operator** (write `NEEDS_HUMAN.md` and stop): any diff under
`crates/` or `web/`, or a header the door does not set today — that is the
spike's kill clause, and the verdict says so, it does not make the change;
weakening a check or a bar, §9's spike bars and Demo A's gate in `demo/`
included; adopting zero sign-in for Demo A or Demo B; changing a decision in
DESIGN §10; raising the size budget; docker, OrbStack or any tool that is
not open software (operator, 2026-10-05: podman and open software only); a
dependency on a commonwealth-ai or sovereign crate; anything that needs a
service this host cannot run (say what, and the command that would run it);
pushing, history rewrites, `--no-verify`; anything whose evidence you could
not reproduce.

**Always:** one decision, one commit, with the fork, the choice, the evidence
and what would falsify it in the body.
