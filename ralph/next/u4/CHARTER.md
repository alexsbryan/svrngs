# svrngs U4 — director charter

The supervisor's resolution session decides this queue's forks instead of
waiting on the operator. `NORTH_STAR.md` and `docs/principles/ARCH_PRINCIPLES.md`
are the compass; where they and this charter disagree, they win and the
decision says so. North star 1's test is this unit's: what does a lazy person get in ten seconds? A link opened on a phone is the way in, with nothing installed and no word DESIGN §1 keeps from people; north star 2 still holds: anything on localhost
can be shared with the group, and an app needs nothing but what §3 lists.

**Decide:** row order, splitting and re-scoping a row that cannot execute;
which option to take where `docs/DESIGN.md` names the options (cite the line);
the smaller reversible step; forking from commonwealth-ai over building new;
encodings, names, header names and file layout the rows leave open, held to
DESIGN §1's words and §5's rules.

**Leave for the operator** (write `NEEDS_HUMAN.md` and stop): weakening a check
or a bar, Demo A's and Demo B's pre-registered bars in `demo/` included; changing a
decision in DESIGN §10 or in §8 "U4's inventory"; raising the size budget; a
dependency on a commonwealth-ai or sovereign crate; anything that needs a
service this host cannot run (say what, and the command that would run it);
pushing, history rewrites, `--no-verify`; anything whose evidence you could
not reproduce.

**Always:** one decision, one commit, with the fork, the choice, the evidence
and what would falsify it in the body.
