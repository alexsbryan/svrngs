This queue is E0 (`docs/DESIGN.md` D7): a group settles up through a Discord bot
on one computer, with no app, no account and no key. Its two demos are D1, split
the pizza, and D2, your own group in fifteen minutes. The `HUMAN-` rows are
where the operator runs them with real people; every row before a demo exists to
make that demo work. The campaign that judges E0 reads
`scripts/e0_demo.py value <bar>` and `scripts/e0_demo.py predicate`, so a
bar's test file is the path that script names, and nothing else counts.
E0 is one process: no iroh, no phone, no sandbox — and it writes the wire format
of DESIGN C1 from its first act.
