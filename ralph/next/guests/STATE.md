# svrngs guests — a member's guest, for a visit, seen by the house (operator, 2026-10-16)

The operator chose the order after V1: "Ok so the queue next is: loopback,
canon, then guest". The design is svrn-docs `rings/current/GUESTS.md`, its
§10 decided. A guest is one person, named, there for a visit, using what
their host lends. The house sees who's visiting, whose guest they are and
until when. A guest never reads the record. A visit ends on time.

**2026-10-18, a lean pass.** The first draft (203ac18b) was GUESTS row by
row. It bet on four things no person has tried: that a member wants a
friend to use their lend for a few days, that the friend arrives through
the grapevine, that the house is fine seeing guests at two per host for 48
hours, and that guests come back and join. Two of its rows stood on layers
no person has used either: the grapevine's people bars never ran
(`GRAPEVINE.md` §7), nor House rules' real house (bar 14,
`demo/runs/canon/2026-10-17-house/`). The competitor is a host's own login
handed to a friend, which carries the guest as the host: the substitution
GUESTS exists to prevent. The operator chose the smallest build that tests
the first and third bets with people:
- "A link in any chat": a visit is made the way a person joins, not through
  the grapevine;
- "+150 repo, +60 core", the small version: every fact of GUESTS §3 kept,
  the bounds fixed at the decided two guests per host and 48 hours, a guest
  opening only their host's own lends;
- "At the run": the queue builds it, runs it on one host and stops.

Then, of the host's **Have a guest until Sunday**: "Let's make that duration
configurable, but with reasonable default (48hr)". Asked how long a host may
choose: "Up to 48 hours". The host chooses each visit's length, 48 hours
chosen to start, and anything shorter allowed; the house's 48 hours stays
the bound until a house asks to change it.

There are eight rows:

1. the canon queue's four open findings, #207-#210, all in `apps/`;
2. an economizing pass;
3. a visit, folded by svrngs-core with its fixed bounds;
4. the door lets a guest in, as a guest, at their host's own lends;
5. a visit made with a link in any chat, and the guest's page;
6. the pages: your guests, who's visiting;
7. a run on one host;
8. a guest let in from their first request (2026-10-20).

**Waits, each with its trigger** (GUESTS §8):
- a lend of a housemate's opened to a guest by its lender's yes: a host asks
  for one, counted;
- the house's two settings, `GuestRules`: a house asks to change its guests
  per host or a visit's longest, counted;
- the settings decided in House rules (CANON_IN_SVRNGS §4): the settings
  land, and a house on svrngs holds its rules in House rules;
- a visit made through the grapevine thread's page: the grapevine's people
  bars pass, or a host asks to have someone from a thread over, counted;
- the words for people, `RELEASES.md` and a `demo/` page: a real visit.

**The measure, the operator's** (proposed 2026-10-18, set before any data):
in the operator's own group over two weeks, a host has someone over twice,
and a guest is watching within 5 minutes of opening the link with nobody
helping. Kill bar: a host hands a friend their own login instead, or nobody
makes a second visit. Then the waits stay waiting, and the shape is looked
at again.
