# svrngs guests — a member's guest, for a visit, seen by the house (operator, 2026-10-16)

The operator chose the order after V1: "Ok so the queue next is: loopback,
canon, then guest". The design is svrn-docs `rings/current/GUESTS.md`
(231df13), its §10 decided. A guest is one person, named, there for a visit,
using what their host lends. The house sees who's visiting, whose guest they
are and until when. A guest never reads the record. A visit ends on time.
There are ten rows:

1. the canon queue's four open findings, #207-#210, all in `apps/`;

3. a visit, and the house's two settings, folded by svrngs-core;
4. the door lets a guest in, as a guest;
5. a lend's "guests welcome";
6. how a visit is made, two taps through the grapevine;
7. the pages: your guests, who's visiting, the settings;
8. the settings as House rules decide them;
9. a run on one host;
10. the words that tell people.

The decisions are in `CHARTER.md`. The ceiling is the operator's, not set
