This queue gives a member a guest, after canon (the operator, 2026-10-16:
"loopback, canon, then guest"). Before a row, read this queue's
`CHARTER.md` and `STATE.md`'s opening, and svrn-docs
`rings/current/GUESTS.md` whole. This is GUESTS's first build only
(GUESTS §10, 2026-10-18): what STATE.md lists under "Waits" is not built
here.

Fences the rows keep:
1. *Instrument and reproduce first* (AGENTS.md 2 and 5). Each change starts
   red, named in its commit, and every new check fails first on a plant it
   names.
2. *A guest is a guest* (GUESTS §3). Every row keeps all nine facts.
   - A guest is always someone's guest; the host's leave or removal ends
     their visits.
   - A visit is an act in the record, never hidden from the house. What the
     guest does on it is not in the record.
   - A guest's key never stands, so it syncs nothing and reads no person,
     presence or record, and is never a lend's reader.
   - A guest opens only their host's own lends. (A lend whose lender
     welcomed guests waits.)
   - Every app is told a guest is a guest, and whose. A guest is never
     carried as their host.
   - A visit ends on time, within 48 hours of its act.
   - Guests are always fewer than members, and each host has at most two.
   - A visit never becomes membership.
   - Each visit's key is fresh, never the key of the guest's own computer.
3. *Folded, not refused* (GUESTS §3.7). A visit past a bound is not a visit
   on any copy; the bounds live in svrngs-core's fold, never in a page.
4. *One decider* (AGENTS.md 8). svrngs-core decides who is visiting, as it
   decides who is a member. The two bounds are each one constant there.
5. *No new office* (GUESTS §4). svrngs adds no role and no policy beyond the
   fixed bounds.
6. *Never widen silently* (WHAT_RUNS_AS_YOU §1). The trust floor
   (`scripts/trust_floor.txt`) holds; any widening of a page's policy or a
   listener is NEEDS_HUMAN.md.
7. *For someone a bit afraid technically.* Each button says what it does
   before it does it. Nothing begun on a page needs the terminal to finish.
8. *The house voice* (svrn-docs `VOICE.md`): second person, active, present.
9. *The computer is the main flow.* The home page comes first, then the
   phone's page.
10. *The budget:* at most 16,925 for the repository and 3,076 for
    svrngs-core (the operator, 2026-10-20, "Cover both rows", carried over
    V1.1.3's own +20; CHARTER.md).
    The economizing pass goes first. Each row raises only what it needs, in its own commit.
    `apps/` is not counted.
11. *Nothing outward-facing.* Every computer and phone runs on this host.

One host, one run of each: not a rate (AGENTS.md 7). A run that could not
finish is could-not-judge, never a pass (AGENTS.md 6).
