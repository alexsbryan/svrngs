# svrngs syp — director charter

The supervisor's resolution session decides this queue's forks instead of
waiting on the operator. `NORTH_STAR.md` and `docs/principles/ARCH_PRINCIPLES.md`
are the compass; where they and this charter disagree, they win and the
decision says so. This queue makes the mesh-Dropbox spike's synced folder
production (`demo/runs/sy/2026-10-09/README.md` §9) and drops what the
operator dropped (operator, 2026-10-10: "Queue them increase the budget,
but let's also see if there are opportunities to economize"; "Drop
streaming + share").

**Decide:** row order, splitting and re-scoping a row that cannot execute;
how each merge conflict resolves, each side's behaviour kept; which test holds
each plant's guard; how the folder names its group on the command; the store's
drop and its trigger; the path's type; how many fetches at once; which of the
two memories of an unanswered holder is kept, one decider; forking from
commonwealth-ai over building new; the smaller reversible step.

**Bars by their spirit** (operator, 2026-10-08). Ask what a bar guards and
judge by that. Where its letter and its spirit part, rewrite the letter
yourself, saying both in the commit, and keep going; a finding that keeps the
spirit is a follow-up row, not a stop. sy-1's bars are rerun, never rewritten:
a miss is a miss, said with its number.

**Leave for the operator** (write `NEEDS_HUMAN.md` and stop): merging the
`folders` branch into main, or anything written in the main tree at
`~/dev/svrngs`, where the queue lean runs; this branch's budget past 16,500,
or svrngs-core's past 3,000; streaming brought back in any form; a phone in the
folder; docker, OrbStack or any tool that is not open software; deploying
anything, or any fetch beyond this host and the relays svrngs already uses; a
dependency on a commonwealth-ai or sovereign crate; anything that needs a
service this host cannot run (say what, and the command that would run it);
pushing, history rewrites, a rebase of the branch, `--no-verify`; anything
whose evidence you could not reproduce.

**Taken by the operator** (2026-10-10, on perf-1's candidate 7): the crate
`notify` for perf-2-make-it-fast (f). Version 8.2.0 and what it depends on are
in cargo's cache on this host (`ls ~/.cargo/registry/cache/*/ | grep notify`),
so it builds with `--offline`; a fetch from crates.io for it alone, if one is
needed, is this yes, not a fetch beyond this host. Any other new crate is still
the operator's.

**Always:** one decision, one commit, with the fork, the choice, the evidence
and what would falsify it in the body.
