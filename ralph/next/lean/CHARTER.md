# svrngs lean — director charter

The supervisor's resolution session decides this queue's forks instead of
waiting on the operator. `NORTH_STAR.md` and `docs/principles/ARCH_PRINCIPLES.md`
are the compass; where they and this charter disagree, they win and the
decision says so. The operator, 2026-10-10: "let's also see if there are
opportunities to economize". Lines are cut here only where the product does
not carry them; nothing a person or an app sees changes in a `lean-` row, and
the `supf-` rows change only what sup-4's run asked.

**Decide:** row order, splitting and re-scoping a row that cannot execute;
where a moved test or a helper lands; whether a test-only item moves into test
support or its test is rewritten; which of a pair is kept, and the shape of the
one; whether a pair is a clone at all — one that decides differently is not;
the one source both pages load for the question and its shape; the
smaller reversible step.

**Bars by their spirit** (operator, 2026-10-08: "the bars are guidance to
align us at the start, the spirit of them are what guides how we
iterate"). Ask what a bar guards and judge by that. Where its letter and
its spirit part, rewrite the letter yourself, saying both in the commit,
and keep going; a finding that keeps the spirit is a follow-up row, not a
stop.

**Leave for the operator** (write `NEEDS_HUMAN.md` and stop): a cut that
changes what a person or an app sees, outside the `supf-` rows; raising the
budget past 15,350, or svrngs-core's past 3,000; deleting a recorded run's
file under `demo/runs/`; anything written in `~/dev/svrngs-folders`, where
the queue syp runs on the branch `folders`; merging `folders` into main;
deploying the page host (`scripts/deploy-page.sh`) or anything else
outward-facing; changing a decision in DESIGN §10; docker, OrbStack or any
tool that is not open software (operator, 2026-10-05); a dependency on a
commonwealth-ai or sovereign crate; anything that needs a service this host
cannot run (say what, and the command that would run it); pushing, history
rewrites, `--no-verify`; anything whose evidence you could not reproduce.

**Always:** one decision, one commit, with the fork, the choice, the evidence
and what would falsify it in the body.
