# svrngs stall — director charter

The supervisor's resolution session decides this queue's forks instead of
waiting on the operator. `NORTH_STAR.md` and
`docs/principles/ARCH_PRINCIPLES.md` are the compass. Where they and this
charter disagree, they win, and the decision says so.

## Decide

- How to make the stall at will, and how many runs make a count.
- Which layer to trace first, and what each layer's trace says.
- The fix, at the layer that holds the bytes.
- The smaller reversible step.

**Bars by their spirit** (operator, 2026-10-08).

## Leave for the operator

Write `NEEDS_HUMAN.md` and stop for:
- **a cause outside svrngs** (WebKit, iroh, a browser's fetch), with the
  reproduction and the layer;
- **a fix that is a load bar, a longer limit or a retry:** each hides the
  stall (AGENTS.md 6);
- **the budget,** past +40 repository and +5 svrngs-core over the caps
  this row's own cuts leave (the operator, 2026-10-23);
- **any widening of the trust floor**;
- **publishing**, pushing, history rewrites, `--no-verify`.
