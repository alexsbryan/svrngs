This queue makes the phone's door an iPhone app, as far as a demo the house
can hold. Before a row, read this queue's `CHARTER.md`, svrn-docs
`rings/current/ROAD_TO_IOS_GA.md` §2 to §4, and the spike's records:
`demo/runs/phone-tunnel/2026-10-26/README.md` and
`demo/runs/phone-replica/2026-10-27/README.md`.

Fences the rows keep:
1. **Instrument and reproduce first** (AGENTS.md 2 and 5). Every gate is
   watched failing on a plant, named in its commit.
2. **The spike lands first, with its behaviour kept,** then changes, in
   its own commits.
3. **One decider** (AGENTS.md 8). Each of these has one home:
   - the reports policy;
   - the idle time;
   - a phone's silence limit;
   - what a kind's phone app is (`well_known.txt`).
4. **No universe to explain** (DESIGN §1). The app's words never say VPN,
   tunnel, key, relay or node; iOS's own prompt is the one place "VPN"
   appears. Every word follows svrn-docs `VOICE.md`'s task register:
   - 30 words a screen before its first action;
   - the same words as the group's page wherever the act is the same.
5. **The house look in iOS's own controls.** That means a system List,
   system type, named colours and hard rules, as the board draws it. No
   custom chrome, and no lend shown inside the app (guideline 4.7).
6. **Never silently substitute** (AGENTS.md 6). Each of these is said in
   the app's words and traced:
   - an asleep lender;
   - a lend that asks a yes;
   - a refused link;
   - a port taken.
7. **Nothing is asked of the person** but a name and iOS's VPN prompt: no
   account, no notifications, no location, no contacts.
8. **The computers stay as they are.** The patches and the phone's
   settings apply only to the phone's build.
9. **Nothing outward-facing.** No upload, no deploy, no upstream issue: each
   is drafted, and the operator's.
