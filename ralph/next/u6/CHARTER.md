# svrngs U6 — director charter

The supervisor's resolution session decides this queue's forks instead of
waiting on the operator. `NORTH_STAR.md` and `docs/principles/ARCH_PRINCIPLES.md`
are the compass; where they and this charter disagree, they win and the
decision says so. North star 1's test is this unit's: what does a lazy person
get in ten seconds? A link opened on a phone, and the film on the TV, with
nothing installed by anyone who came for the evening; north star 2 still
holds: anything on localhost can be shared with the group, and the room door
serves any lend the same way.

**Decide:** row order, splitting and re-scoping a row that cannot execute;
which option to take where `docs/DESIGN.md` names the options (cite the line);
the smaller reversible step; forking from commonwealth-ai over building new;
the room door's command name, encodings, header names and file layout the
rows leave open, held to DESIGN §1's words and §5's rules.

**Leave for the operator** (write `NEEDS_HUMAN.md` and stop): weakening a check
or a bar, Demo B's pre-registered gate in `demo/` and §8 included; changing a
decision in DESIGN §10 or in §8 "U6's inventory"; anything that would put a
screen, player, AirPlay, Cast or media word or protocol into svrngs (operator,
2026-10-05: out of scope); a door behaviour that only one app needs; raising
the size budget; a dependency on a commonwealth-ai or sovereign crate;
anything that needs a service this host cannot run (say what, and the command
that would run it); pushing, history rewrites, `--no-verify`; anything whose
evidence you could not reproduce.

**Always:** one decision, one commit, with the fork, the choice, the evidence
and what would falsify it in the body.
