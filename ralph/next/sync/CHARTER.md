# svrngs sync — director charter

The supervisor's resolution session decides this queue's forks instead of
waiting on the operator. `NORTH_STAR.md` and `docs/principles/ARCH_PRINCIPLES.md`
are the compass; where they and this charter disagree, they win and the
decision says so. This queue is a spike: it answers whether a group's folder
can sync both ways on its members' disks — a mesh Dropbox — by one run against
bars set before any data, and ends in a verdict. How it becomes production is
the operator's (operator, 2026-10-09: "The potential was far beyond just that
(basically a whole mesh dropbox concept)"; "Draft that spike next (as tight
as possible)").

**Decide:** the smallest slice that answers the bars; where the folder's
index lives (iroh-docs, which the inventory found fits — or svrngs's record —
said which and why, with one source for who may read); how a conflict is
named on disk; how the run drives the nodes and partitions them; forking from
commonwealth-ai over building new; the smaller reversible step.

**Bars by their spirit** (operator, 2026-10-08). Ask what a bar guards and
judge by that. A bar is never rewritten after its data exists: a miss is a
miss, said with its number.

**Leave for the operator** (write `NEEDS_HUMAN.md` and stop): merging the
`folders` branch into main, or anything written in the main tree at
`~/dev/svrngs`, where scoped use's loop runs; this branch's budget past
15,750, or svrngs-core's past 3,000; changing a bar or a prediction after
sy-1's first commit; docker, OrbStack or any tool that is not open software;
deploying anything, or any fetch beyond this host and the relays svrngs
already uses; a dependency on a commonwealth-ai or sovereign crate; anything
that needs a service this host cannot run (say what, and the command that
would run it); pushing, history rewrites, `--no-verify`; anything whose
evidence you could not reproduce.

**Always:** one decision, one commit, with the fork, the choice, the evidence
and what would falsify it in the body.
