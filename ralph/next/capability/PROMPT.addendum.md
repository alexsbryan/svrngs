This queue holds svrngs to one rule, by capability: what can act as a person
is what they or their group chose, never widened silently (svrn-docs
`rings/current/WHAT_CAN_ACT_AS_YOU.md` §1, §2). Before a row, read this
queue's `CHARTER.md`, `STATE.md`'s opening, and that note whole.

Fences the rows keep:
1. *Red first, as the protection* (AGENTS.md 2, 5). Each row's first test is
   that the protection holds, failing at HEAD. Describe what is refused and
   why in the test and the commit. A commit body is no place for a how-to of
   what it closes.
2. *Nothing a person does today breaks, and no new step blocks them.* Each
   change keeps every legitimate path working, and a test names each one it
   keeps: opening a lend from the home page or the group page; a lend's own
   page moving within itself; a phone's yes through the group page; Events'
   and Money's own corrections by their authors; a newcomer opening Money
   and Notes answering no question. Once capability-scenes lands, the
   gate's "scenes" step holds every demo scene too: a row that breaks one
   does not close.
3. *Capabilities, not buckets.* A rule says what code can do as a person,
   never what kind of app it is.
4. *The decider is one place* (AGENTS.md 8). A check lives where the decision
   already lives: the door's, the fold's, the app's. Never a second copy
   beside it.
5. *Glassbox* (AGENTS.md 1). Every refusal traces at debug and names the fact
   that refused it. The person is told in words they can act on.
6. *Old acts still verify.* No row changes how an act already written
   verifies or folds; the golden tests hold it.
7. *The budget:* the economizing pass first, and a raise is the operator's.
   `apps/` is not counted.
8. *The trust floor* (`scripts/trust_floor.txt`) is only ever narrowed here.
   A widening is NEEDS_HUMAN.md.
9. *Publishing is the operator's.* The last row prepares a release and stops.
10. *Scratch stays under `target/`.* A clone, a run's output or a check's
    log goes under `target/`, never `/tmp`: the settings refuse `rm -rf`
    outside the tree, and two sessions lost turns to it.
11. *A gate's result needs no commit of its own.* The await log keeps it;
    cite that log in the row's last commit or a run's README. The settings
    refuse `git commit --allow-empty`.
