# svrngs middleware made production — director charter

The supervisor's resolution session decides this queue's forks instead of
waiting on the operator. `NORTH_STAR.md` and `docs/principles/ARCH_PRINCIPLES.md`
are the compass; where they and this charter disagree, they win and the
decision says so. The test is the operator's, 2026-10-08: does the library
carry the weight? An app's middleware holds only that app's nouns, and the
door does the rest for any app.

**Decide:** row order, splitting and re-scoping a row that cannot execute;
the hooks' contract where DESIGN leaves it open — the flag's name, the
route's shape, encodings, header names, the stand-in, file layout — held to
DESIGN §1's words and §5's rules; whether the script goes in by the door or
at the lend's origin, by the data; which second app proves the hooks;
forking from commonwealth-ai over building new; the smaller reversible step.

**Bars by their spirit** (operator, 2026-10-08: "the bars are guidance to
align us at the start, the spirit of them are what guides how we
iterate"). Ask what a bar guards and judge by that. Where its letter and
its spirit part, rewrite the letter yourself, saying both in the commit,
and keep going; a finding that keeps the spirit is a follow-up row, not a
stop.

**Leave for the operator** (write `NEEDS_HUMAN.md` and stop): an app's noun
in the infra, or a hook only one app could use; a change against a bar's
spirit; a lend with no hook behaving differently; raising the size budget
past 14,400; changing a decision in DESIGN §10; docker, OrbStack or any tool
that is not open software (operator, 2026-10-05: podman and open software
only); a dependency on a commonwealth-ai or sovereign crate; anything that
needs a service this host cannot run (say what, and the command that would
run it); pushing, history rewrites, `--no-verify`; anything whose evidence
you could not reproduce.

**Always:** one decision, one commit, with the fork, the choice, the evidence
and what would falsify it in the body.
