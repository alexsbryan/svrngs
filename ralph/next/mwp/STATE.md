# svrngs middleware, made production — the door carries what every app's middleware would write (docs/DESIGN.md §8 "Then"; §3 "The room" call 6; §10, 2026-10-08)

The spike kept Jellyfin's zero sign-in as a middleware with no line of
svrngs changed (`demo/runs/mw/2026-10-08/`), and so the library carried
none of it: of the middleware's 1,043 lines of code, about 600 are any
app's (b764b6a's count). This ring moves them into the door as hooks a
lend declares over HTTP — a credential per person, that credential kept
out of answers, a script first in the lend's pages — and leaves the app
its own nouns. The bar, in its spirit: Jellyfin plays with nothing typed
as it did in mw-2, through a middleware that is only Jellyfin's part, and
a second app signs each person in through the same hooks with no line of
the door's knowing it. The budget is 14,400 for it (b764b6a, §5).
