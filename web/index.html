  How this reaches you
  
    The link from the chat keeps its code after the #, which your browser never sends to the site this page comes from.
    
  
    This browser keeps who you are. There is no account anywhere.
    
  
    The site this page comes from sends only files. It does see which thing’s address you open, as each has a name under it. Your group can serve this page from a site of its own.
    
  
    Between you and the computer lending a thing, a server passes it along sealed, and can’t read it. Your group can run its own.
    
  
    That computer is someone’s in your group. It checks it’s you before it answers, and tells the app there who you are: some apps let you straight in, others ask you to sign in once.
    
  
    What your group keeps together is on its people’s own computers, and can leave with the group, whole.
