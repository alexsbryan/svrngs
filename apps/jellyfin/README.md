# Jellyfin

For whoever lends Jellyfin. svrngs finds it by its own port, 8096:
`svrngs share 8096`, or lend it from the home page under "Already running
here". It is lent as it comes, with no password typed, so Jellyfin asks
each person to sign in once, with an account you make them; `share` tells
you how, and the catalogue's run measured it on one computer
(`demo/runs/catalogue/2026-10-05/`).

**Sign in with svrngs.** With Jellyfin's SSO plugin installed and pointing
at this computer, lend it with `svrngs share 8096 --sign-in-with-svrngs`,
or turn it on from the home page. Then nobody signs in: each person who
opens Jellyfin from the group's list is in as themselves, on a computer or
a phone, with nothing typed, and two people watching at once stay apart.
The plugin is Jellyfin's own; svrngs learns nothing of it. Its settings and
the run that measured them are `demo/runs/oidc/2026-10-21-jellyfin-2/`.
