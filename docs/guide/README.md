# The svrngs guide

svrngs keeps your group's things on your group's computers. Someone sends a
link in the chat, you open it and give your name, and you're in. Anything
running on one person's computer can then be opened by everyone else in the
group, from a phone or a computer, with no accounts and no passwords.

New here? Read [How it works](how-it-works.md) (five minutes), then
[Start a group](start.md). Then pick what you came for.

## Three things to know before you start

**A computer lends; a phone opens.** A phone joins in its browser and opens
everything the group lends. Lending, and keeping the group's copy, takes a
Mac or a Linux computer with svrngs on it.

**Your computer has to be on.** What you lend is served from your computer.
When it sleeps, your friends see that it didn't answer, and it comes back
when you do. A game kept with [Give](games.md) is the exception: another
computer in the group can take it over.

**It's early.** [`RELEASES.md`](../../RELEASES.md) says what works today and
what doesn't yet.
