## (a) and (b)

(a) today: each copy's tar, whole, to each of 20 members, 12 copies an hour. Nominal: every tar moves. Measured: a tar byte-identical to the one before moves nothing (files move by hash), so only the tars that differed in this run count.
(b) the fix: per copy, the changed and added files plus the manifest (only when anything changed), to each of 3 holders. Idle: the mean of the five idle windows, × 12 an hour. Light use: the one window that held it, in MB per holder (one use costs this once).
