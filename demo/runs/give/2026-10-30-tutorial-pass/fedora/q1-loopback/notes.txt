Q1: a program that listens on 127.0.0.1 only, inside a rootless container on
Fedora (podman 5.8.7, netavark, pasta 0^20260728.gf8df3f1-2.fc44), published
with -p 127.0.0.1:<hostport>:8000. Run 2026-10-30 ~09:52 PDT by q1.sh (here).

$ podman info | grep -iE 'networkBackend|rootlessNetworkCmd'

Program: docker.io/library/python@sha256:1148dbb0...dd0b (python:3.12-slim),

pasta:--host-lo-to-ns-lo bind=127.0.0.1: http=200 size=832

Why the reset (from /proc/net/tcp inside default-any during the curl):
  0C01A8C0:1F40 <- 0C01A8C0:939E   i.e. 192.168.1.12:8000 from 192.168.1.12
pasta copies the host's address into the container's namespace and delivers
a forwarded connection to that address, not to the namespace's loopback; a
socket bound to 127.0.0.1 never sees it. (bridge: the container's 10.88.0.3,
0300580A, same result.)

pasta --help:
  --host-lo-to-ns-lo	Translate host-loopback forwards to namespace loopback
With it (`--network pasta:--host-lo-to-ns-lo`) the loopback-only program
answers 200 with no forwarder. A card can't use it: card.rs refuses every
`network_mode` (card.rs:296-302).

Teardown note: `python3 -m http.server` as PID 1 ignores SIGTERM; podman rm -f
fell back to SIGKILL after 10 s for each (expected, not a finding).
