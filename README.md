# svrngs

svrngs lets friends open what you’re running on localhost. Share Jellyfin,
a model, or the thing you vibe coded last night. I wanted the feeling of localhost, but expanded to a community.

Your friends can run their own cloud P2P. The group decides who gets in and svrngs
manages the files and records on the shared computers. 

## Start a group

In Terminal on a Mac or Linux computer:

```sh
curl -fsSL https://group.svrnme.sh/install.sh | sh -s -- 'https://group.svrnme.sh/'
```

Make a group and drop the invite link in your chat. Friends can join in their
phone’s browser. Add some of our exmple apps like **Money** to split the pizza bill, or share an app from **Already running here**.
